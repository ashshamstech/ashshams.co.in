Welcome to Basera PG and Hostel

We're committed to providing a comfortable and affordable living experience for students and professionals.

Story & History

Started in 2015, Basera PG and Hostel has been dedicated to providing quality accommodation for students and working professionals.

Goal & Mission

Our goal is to provide a safe, clean, and comfortable living environment for all our residents. We strive to create a community where everyone feels at home.

Target & Success

We aim to achieve a 100% satisfaction rate among our residents by continuously improving our services and facilities.

Our Services

We're dedicated to providing top-notch services to our residents, ensuring their comfort and satisfaction. From housekeeping to food and laundry, we've got it all covered.

Meals Included

Home-style vegetarian breakfast, lunch and dinner served daily, with a changing weekly menu.

Laundry

Regular laundry service so you never have to plan your week around washing clothes.

Housekeeping

Rooms and shared areas cleaned on a fixed schedule, with bedding changed regularly.

High-Speed Internet

Unlimited Wi-Fi across every property, fast enough for classes, calls and remote work.

24x7 Security

Round-the-clock caretaker presence, CCTV coverage and secured entry at all locations.

Power Backup

Backup power so lights, fans and internet keep running through any outage.
